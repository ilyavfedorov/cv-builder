# Job Screening: University of Auckland — Student Experience Adviser

**Verdict: GO**

## Summary
Inna is a credible fit for this Student Experience Adviser role. The core of the role — planning and delivering events, programmes, and engagement initiatives to connect people with support systems and build a sense of belonging — maps directly to her strengths in programme coordination, community-facing communication, and relationship building. Her experience creating student-centred environments (Marie International School nursery and primary levels, after-school care at Takapuna) shows she understands how to help people feel connected and supported. Gaps exist around tertiary sector experience and explicit knowledge of NZ equity frameworks, but neither is an absolute dealbreaker given the strength of her coordination and engagement background. The salary range ($63,600–$79,300) is competitive and the fulltime permanent structure represents a meaningful step up.

- Extensive experience planning and delivering programmes, events, and engagement initiatives in education settings
