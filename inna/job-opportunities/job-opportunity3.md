This is not a traditional Executive Assistant or Office Administrator role. You'll sit at the intersection of executive support, office operations, and process improvement, helping keep priorities moving while constantly asking "how could this work better?"

At its core, this role involves hands-on executive support for a busy Founder/CEO; managing diary, inbox, travel, and meeting preparation while also owning day-to-day office operations and support across the wider team. From there, the opportunity opens up to improve how things run, introducing better systems, smarter processes, and efficiencies as you go.

We're looking for someone who can work at pace, is highly detail oriented, and can pivot quickly as priorities evolve. You'll be someone who moves fast, thinks big, and values people who take ownership, think ahead, and act without waiting to be asked.

If you're curious, sharp, tech‑comfortable (especially with AI), and motivated by having real visibility into how decisions are made, this could be the role for you.

The role
Day to day, you will:
Act as a key administrative support partner to a busy founder/CEO, managing priorities, protecting focus time, and anticipating what's coming next
Own diary, inbox flow, meeting prep and follow-ups
Coordinate projects, events, board & leadership meetings, and key initiatives
Support onboarding/offboarding of new starters and ensure a strong internal experience for team members
Work with the wider team to help implement and adopt tools, systems and AI to reduce friction and increase speed
Leave every process better than you found it
What were looking for:
You don't need to tick every box, but you do need the right mindset
Can work at pace, pivot quickly, and stay effective when things aren't perfectly defined
Is curious and enjoys learning and adopting new tools, systems, and technology (including AI)
Has excellent organisation, attention to detail, and project management skills
Communicates clearly and confidently and can share ideas with all levels of the business
