# Job Screening: Action Education — Programme Coordinator

**Verdict: GO**

## Summary

Inna is a strong fit for this Programme Coordinator role. Her core strengths — coordinating complex multi-stakeholder programmes, managing cross-functional logistics, executing events end-to-end, and building relationships across diverse groups — directly match the primary requirements of this role. Her international background and multicultural communication experience align well with Action Education's community-facing, people-centred mahi. While she lacks explicit Te Tiriti o Waitangi grounding and direct youth development experience in a NZ community context, this role is fundamentally a coordination and delivery role, and those skills are comprehensively evidenced. The part-time hours and community-driven kaupapa are a good structural fit for someone establishing themselves in Auckland's non-profit sector.

- 10+ years of programme and event coordination experience, including large-scale events (150+ attendees, 20+ staff) at Marie International School

- Strong administrative backbone: documentation management, scheduling, compliance registers, reporting

- Demonstrated invoicing and expense tracking experience (self-employed period), relevant to Xero/invoicing requirement
- Multilingual communicator (English, Japanese, Russian) with extensive experience in multicultural settings
- Experience working directly with children and young people at Takapuna Primary School (after-school care, arts and music activities)
- Hands-on event creation from scratch: venue selection, scripting, organisation, and hosting at Marie International School
- Confidence with digital tools: Microsoft Office, Instagram, ClassDojo, survey tools

- No direct experience with spoken word poetry, arts-based youth development, or the specific creative facilitation style Action Education uses
- Full NZ driver licence not evidenced on CV
