Based in Sandringham, community delivery across Tāmaki Makaurau

He reo rangatahi, he ao hurihuri — the voices of youth keep the world turning.
At Action Education, we believe everyone has a story to tell, and that creativity and self‑expression are powerful forces for wellbeing, connection, and change. If you’re someone who thrives in community, values kaupapa‑driven mahi, and loves supporting young people to find and use their voice, this role might be your next step.

We’re looking for a Programmes Coordinator who can bring heart, organisation, and energy to the delivery of our programmes and events across schools, communities, and partner organisations.

About the Role

As our Programmes Coordinator, you’ll help create the conditions where rangatahi can show up as themselves — without judgement — and explore who they are through creative expression. You’ll plan and coordinate programmes, support facilitators, manage logistics, and help bring events and community activations to life.

Your mahi will help ensure our spaces are safe, accessible, and grounded in our commitment to Te Tiriti o Waitangi. You’ll also nurture relationships with schools, community groups, funders, and partners, helping our programmes remain responsive, relevant, and impactful.

This role is varied, people‑focused, and deeply connected to the heart of Action Education.

What You’ll Do

Coordinate and support delivery of programmes across schools and community settings.

Plan and deliver events, workshops, and community activations that reflect our kaupapa.

Manage logistics including bookings, schedules, resources, and health & safety.

Build strong relationships with schools, community organisations, and stakeholders.

Act as the first point of contact for programme and event enquiries.

Maintain accurate data, reporting, and records for programmes and funding contracts.

Support invoicing processes and provide basic assistance in Xero.

Contribute to continuous improvement, team culture, and wider organisational initiatives.

Uphold Action Education’s values, bicultural commitments, and youth‑centred practice.

What You Bring

Experience working in diverse community settings.

Strong communication skills and the ability to build rapport with diverse groups.

Experience coordinating programmes, events, or community initiatives.

Excellent organisational and administrative skills.

Confidence using Microsoft Office and digital tools.

Experience with scheduling, reporting, or funding contracts (advantageous).

Familiarity with Xero or invoicing processes (advantageous).

Who You Are

Passionate about youth development, creativity, and community wellbeing.

Culturally responsive, grounded in Te Tiriti o Waitangi, and committed to equity.

Warm, engaging, and confident in group settings.

Organised, reliable, and able to juggle multiple priorities.

Reflective, open to feedback, and always learning.

Comfortable working independently and collaboratively.

Other Details

Relevant qualification in youth work, social services, education, community development, event management, or equivalent experience (preferred).

Full NZ driver licence (preferred).

Flexibility to work evenings and weekends as needed.

Police Vetting required under the Vulnerable Children’s Act.

Why Action Education?

Action Ed is Aotearoa New Zealand’s leading arts-based youth development organisation, structured around the medium of spoken word poetry. We believe creativity and our ability to express who we are is fundamental to our wellbeing.

Our programmes are grounded in creativity, storytelling, and community, supporting young people to build confidence, resilience, and a sense of belonging.

You’ll join a passionate, supportive team committed to making a real difference in the lives of young people across Aotearoa.
