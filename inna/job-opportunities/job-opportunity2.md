He whakamārama mō te tūranga mahi | About the role

We currently have an exciting opportunity for a Job Coordinator to join our Maintenance Services team in Māngere. This is a hybrid dispatch and administrative role, requiring strong coordination capability alongside high-level administrative support to ensure efficient and timely service delivery.


Key duties will include:


Coordinate and dispatch field crews and contractors for planned and reactive works, ensuring priority jobs are allocated appropriately.
Monitor incoming work requests, faults, and urgent jobs, triaging and dispatching resources in line with service priorities and SLAs.
Providing feedback to field supervisors and operations managers on anticipated resource levels and potential need for sub‑contractor resources.
Work scheduling and alignment of available skilled resources to achieve optimal work completion and operating efficiencies.
Provide resource coordination across internal teams and external contractors to ensure all service delivery requirements are met.
Ensure effective communication and escalation with client representatives, including Duty Engineers and the Faults Centre.
Maintain accurate job records, scheduling data, and documentation within job management systems.
Ensure customer and stakeholder needs remain front of mind in all dispatch, scheduling, and administrative activities.
General administrative duties as required.

Ngā pūkenga e rapu nei mātou | About you

To be successful in this role, you will demonstrate:
Experience in a fast‑paced coordination, dispatch, or administrative role, ideally within a maintenance, utilities, or services environment.
Strong administrative capability, with proven ability to manage systems, documentation, and scheduling accurately and efficiently.
Excellent organisational skills and the ability to manage competing priorities in a dynamic dispatch environment.
A service‑focused mindset, highly responsive to deadlines, urgent work, and operational demands.
A process‑orientated approach with high attention to detail and accuracy.
Clear and confident communication skills when working with field crews, supervisors, contractors, and stakeholders.
Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to build effective relationships within a small team and across the wider organisation.
