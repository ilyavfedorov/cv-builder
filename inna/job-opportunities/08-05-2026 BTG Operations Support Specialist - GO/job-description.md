Business Technology Group (BTG) is a nationwide ICT solutions provider delivering consulting, managed services and support to over 400 businesses across NZ and Australia. With 100 staff and strong growth, we pride ourselves on a collaborative culture and exceptional client retention.

Due to growth, we’re looking for a mid-level Operational Support Specialist to support an existing senior team member across service delivery, licensing, billing, and operational coordination.

This is not an entry-level admin role, it’s a hands-on operational position where you’ll take ownership, improve processes, and become a key connector across Sales, Engineering, Finance and vendors.



The Role

You’ll work across:

MSP licensing administration

Billing lifecycle support (quote → invoice → validation)

Subject Matter Expert (SME) in BTG Products & Services

Quoting, procurement and vendor coordination

Administrative provisioning of telecommunication services

End-of-month invoicing assistance

Project and operational coordination

Reviewing billing data and resolving discrepancies

Supporting engineering teams with service onboarding

Identifying process improvements and automation opportunities



About You

You’re organised, commercially aware, and comfortable operating in a technical services environment.

You’ll likely have:

3–5+ years in operations, service delivery, finance support or IT coordination

Strong attention to detail and high accuracy

Experience with quoting, billing or licensing processes

Good Excel skills (intermediate–advanced)

Confidence communicating with engineers, vendors and clients

A proactive mindset and ability to improve processes


Advantageous:

IT or Managed Services experience (MSP)

Microsoft licensing exposure

Xero or ERP system experience

Procurement or vendor management experience



Career Progression

This role is designed as a growth pathway. As capability develops, you’ll have the opportunity to:

Take ownership of specific service lines

Lead licensing and billing governance

Step into project coordination responsibilities

Move toward Operations Lead or Project Management pathways
