Company & Team

If working for a small, private, generous and incredibly successful company is your thing and you enjoy supporting people while running the office and working frontline, then this could be your next opportunity. About the Role

This is a varied and hands-on position where you are the backbone of the office. You’ll balance executive support, office management, accounts coordination and frontline responsibilities.

Executive & Administrative Support

•    Provide high-level administrative support to senior Managers
•    Prepare documentation, reports and correspondence
•    Coordinate meetings, diaries and travel arrangements
•    Assist with project administration and follow-ups
•    Maintain strict confidentiality across all matters

Office Management

•    Oversee daily operations of a boutique office environment
•    Manage suppliers, facilities and office services
•    Ensure the office runs smoothly, professionally and efficiently
•    Maintain systems, processes and filing

Accounts Administration

•    Process invoices and expenses
•    Assist with basic accounts coordination
•    Liaise with external accountants where required
•    Support financial documentation preparation

Marketing Administration

•    Assist with preparation of marketing collateral and presentations
•    Update website or marketing materials as required
•    Coordinate branded documents and templates
•    Support event coordination and stakeholder communications
•    Maintain consistency across company documentation and branding

Front Desk & Team Support

•    Act as the welcoming face of the business
•    Manage incoming calls and visitors
•    Support ad hoc team requirements
•    Be the “glue” that keeps everything connected

About You

•    To add immediate value, you will bring:
•    Previous experience as an Office Manager, Senior Administrator or Personal Assistant

•    The ability to work autonomously in a small team
