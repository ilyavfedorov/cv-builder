About the Role

This role plays a key part in supporting the successful delivery of projects by coordinating schedules, documentation, resources, and stakeholder communication. Working closely with operational leaders and delivery teams, you will ensure projects remain on track, risks are managed, and reporting is accurate while maintaining strong governance and project standards.

Key Responsibilities:
Coordinate project schedules, documentation, reporting, and stakeholder communication to support project delivery.
Monitor milestones, risks, and issues while escalating where appropriate.
Support project governance processes and ensure consistent project standards are followed.
Liaise with internal teams, customers, vendors, and contractors to maintain alignment across project activities.
Maintain accurate and audit-ready project documentation and reporting.
Key Skills and Experience:
Ideally 2+ years’ experience within a service industry, customer service, or service delivery environment.
Previous experience in a Project Coordinator or project support role.
Strong organisational and time management skills with the ability to manage competing priorities.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills with strong stakeholder engagement ability.
Proficiency across Microsoft Office, collaboration platforms, and modern project management tools.
Strong IT fluency and understanding of project delivery environments.
