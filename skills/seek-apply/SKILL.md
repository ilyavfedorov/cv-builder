# SEEK Apply

Handle a SEEK application from vacancy review through a verified outcome while preserving the distinction between a draft, a SEEK status, and an employer-confirmed submission.

## Candidate context

Identify the candidate folder and its authoritative structured CV data before drafting answers. Use the exact vacancy folder for its analysis, tailored CV, cover letter, source URL, and status. If the project contains multiple candidate folders and the intended candidate is unclear, ask which one to use.

Treat the live advert and form as authoritative for application requirements. Match application materials by employer, title, and canonical job URL; similar titles are insufficient.

## Workflow

1. Read the complete vacancy, including employer questions and linked descriptions. Record material requirements or closing dates in the opportunity folder when useful.
2. Follow the Apply flow, including an external employer redirect. A SEEK “applied” or “visited employer site” marker does not prove the employer received an application.
3. Inspect the full form before entering data. Identify required documents, declarations, eligibility, notice period, compensation, optional questions, and the final submission control.
4. Use only verified candidate evidence. Select the vacancy’s exact tailored CV and create a truthful, role-specific cover letter when it is required or materially helpful. Keep gaps, scores, and verification questions out of candidate-facing documents.
5. Ask for facts that authoritative project data cannot establish. Do not infer compensation expectations, notice period, relocation, licences, declarations, or demographic answers.
6. Before sharing private contact data or uploading files, obtain any confirmation required at action time and name the destination and data or files involved.
7. Populate verified answers. Leave optional demographic questions, talent-pool consent, profile-promotion options, and similar marketing choices blank unless the candidate chooses them.
8. Check any SEEK profile information bundled with the application against the authoritative CV. Correct or escalate material conflicts before submission; do not silently publish conflicting history.
9. Stop at the final submission control until the candidate explicitly authorises that prepared application. Do not solve CAPTCHAs.
10. After submission, verify an employer confirmation page, receipt, or application reference. Record the vacancy as draft, ready for review, submitted, failed, or withdrawn.

## Completion report

State the vacancy, documents and material answers used; whether the employer confirmed receipt; and any decision or verification still required.
