# Tanya's tailored job opportunities

The Monday and Thursday job-market task creates one dated folder for every newly discovered role that scores at least 80/100 and does not fail a hard salary or location gate.

Each opportunity folder contains:

- `job-description.md`: source vacancy text and URL.
- `analysis.md`: score, evidence, unknowns, calm-work risks and positioning choice.
- `tanya-poletaeva-<company>-<role>-cv-tailored.md`: editable tailored CV.
- `tanya-poletaeva-<company>-<role>-cv-tailored.pdf`: application-ready PDF draft.

CV creation does not mean the role is approved for application. Roles with unresolved salary, office, on-call or workload questions remain `Research first`. No application or recruiter contact is automatic.

All claims must come from `../cv-data.json`. Tailoring may select, reorder and rephrase supported evidence, but must never invent experience, metrics, technologies or qualifications.
