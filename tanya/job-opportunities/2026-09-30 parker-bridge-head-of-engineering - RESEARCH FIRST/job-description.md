# Parker Bridge — Head of Engineering

- **Source:** https://nz.seek.com/job/94903531
- **Location:** Auckland CBD (hybrid)
- **Type:** Full time
- **Salary:** NZD 210,000–220,000

Own the engineering function and technical direction for an established technology organisation bringing capability in-house. The current team is approximately 12 and expected to grow. Responsibilities span mature business-critical products, new cloud-native products, engineering structure and capability, architecture, quality, DevOps, CI/CD, observability, reliability, security, external partners and AI-assisted engineering.

The role does not require day-to-day production coding but requires technical depth, incident involvement and the ability to lead through other leaders.
