# Opportunity analysis: Sharesies Engineering Manager

## Decision

**Research first** — tailored CV prepared at Tanya's request; application is not yet authorised.

## Fit assessment

The role aligns strongly with Tanya's engineering-management direction. Her best evidence is current leadership of 6 engineers, quarterly planning and prioritisation, high-trust culture building, capability development, CI/CD modernisation and practical adoption of AI-assisted workflows. Her earlier Flintfox Team Lead role adds explicit budget, scope, risk, performance and recruitment evidence, while First Software demonstrates operational ownership across a cloud ERP platform serving 75+ New Zealand and Australian clients.

The advert's vendor-contract requirement is only a partial match. Tanya has extensive customer, implementation and integration leadership, but the canonical CV data does not establish direct contract-negotiation ownership. The tailored CV therefore emphasises relevant commercial and operational judgement without claiming vendor-contract experience.

## Hard-gate status

- **Salary:** Unknown. Verify that base salary can reach at least NZD 160,000, preferably NZD 170,000–180,000.
- **Location:** High risk. The advert says Wellington Central hybrid; verify whether the role can be performed remotely from Auckland with no more than 3 office days and no regular Wellington commute.
- **On-call:** Unknown. Central operational ownership may include incidents or after-hours escalation.
- **Role shape:** Provisional fit. Confirm team size, direct reports, hands-on expectations and the proportion of vendor/budget administration.
- **Calm work:** Unknown. Confirm concurrent priorities, interruption load, recurring meetings and expectations during production incidents.

## Recruiter questions before applying

1. Can the role be based in Auckland or remotely elsewhere in New Zealand, and what office attendance is expected in practice?
2. What is the base-salary range, and is employer KiwiSaver additional?
3. How large is the team, which disciplines report to the manager, and what outcomes define success in the first 6 months?
4. What proportion of the role is people leadership, technical direction, operational ownership, vendor management and budget administration?
5. Does the role carry an on-call roster, after-hours escalation or incident-command responsibility?
6. How many concurrent initiatives does the team normally own, and how are priorities protected when urgent work arrives?
7. What recurring meetings are expected each week?
8. What authority does the manager have to reduce work in progress, improve delivery practices and adopt AI tooling safely?

## Positioning choice

**Primary:** Engineering Manager.

**Supporting narrative:** A people-centred, technically credible leader who builds capable teams and dependable operational systems. The CV leads with Tanya's current 6-person engineering leadership, structured planning, team culture, AI workflow design and CI/CD improvement, supported by earlier budget, delivery and enterprise-platform ownership.
