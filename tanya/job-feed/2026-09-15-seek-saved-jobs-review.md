The documented interests matter: SQL and people leadership are both 9/10 interest; C# coding is 4/10. A technically plausible development role may therefore be less satisfying than a leadership role. These are prompts for discussion, not permanent limits on Tanya's options.

Hard gates: NZD 160,000 base floor; Auckland or NZ-compatible remote arrangement, at most three office days; no routine on-call/after-hours or escalation-led work; manageable responsibilities. Unknown is not a pass. SEEK's personalised salary-match badge is not an advertised salary range. Hybrid is not proof of the number of office days. Culture/benefit claims are positive signals to investigate, not verified workload evidence.

No live role has enough published evidence to pass every hard gate conclusively. The shortlist is therefore **Research first**, with the aim of deciding which applications deserve effort. No artificial numerical ranking is used where salary, hours and workload evidence is missing. An absent skill below means absent from the current verified record, not proof Tanya has never used it.
