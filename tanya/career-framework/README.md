# Tanya's career operating framework

## Outcome

Find a well-paid role that makes Tanya's strongest capabilities unusually useful without trading away sustainable working conditions.

## Success definition

- **Absolute base-salary floor:** NZD 160,000.
- **Income-goal floor:** approximately NZD 168,100 with no KiwiSaver employee deduction, or NZD 177,400 with a 3.5% deduction.
- **Preferred search floor:** NZD 175,000-180,000 base unless the role has exceptional calm-work evidence.
- **Work pattern:** permanent or contract; remote, or hybrid with no more than three Auckland office days; North Shore preferred, Auckland CBD acceptable.
- **Work experience:** clear ownership, feasible workload, limited interruptions and meetings, protected focus and low concurrent work in progress.
- **Role shape:** senior individual contributor, principal, technical leadership or people management.

## Operating rhythm

| Cadence | Activity | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Monday and Thursday | Search, deduplicate, score and investigate new vacancies | `../job-feed/current-jobs.csv` and `../job-feed/scored-jobs.csv` |
| As opportunities appear | Apply the decision gate and recruiter screen | Recorded decision and unresolved verification gates |
| Friday | Capture new learning and achievement evidence | Proposed additions to `../cv-data.json` |
| First Friday monthly | Review the market and adjust strategy | A dated copy of `monthly-review.md` |
| After every recruiter conversation | Record market intelligence | `recruiter-intelligence.csv` |
| Monthly | Count recurring skill gaps and choose at most one development bet | `skills-gap-tracker.csv` |

## Files

- `application-decision-gate.md`: consistent go/hold/decline decisions.
- `calm-work-screen.md`: recruiter and hiring-manager questions with warning signs.
- `positioning-briefs.md`: three truthful CV narratives for different role families.
- `weekly-evidence-capture.md`: converts weekly work into auditable CV evidence.
- `learning-log.md`: dated source evidence captured through weekly check-ins.
- `recruiter-intelligence.csv`: salary, work-pattern and employer intelligence.
- `skills-gap-tracker.csv`: prevents reacting to one-off technology requirements.
- `salary-model.md`: salary thresholds and assumptions.
- `monthly-review.md`: monthly strategy review template.

## Decision rule

Do not optimise for the largest number of applications. Optimise for a small number of roles that pass the hard gates, make strong use of Tanya's differentiators and remain credible after calm-work verification.
