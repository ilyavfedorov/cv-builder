# CV positioning briefs

These are selection guides, not permission to change facts. Every claim must trace to `../cv-data.json`.

## Principal / Lead Engineer

**Promise:** Tanya solves complex enterprise software and data problems while raising engineering clarity and capability around her.

Lead with:
- 20 years across software development, enterprise products and technical leadership.
- C#, ASP.NET, SQL, integrations, legacy-system analysis and modernisation.
- CI/CD modernisation from Jenkins toward AWS-based pipelines.
- Engineering standards, code review, estimation and solution design.
- Mentoring and technical direction without requiring broad programme ownership.

Best-fit titles: Principal Engineer, Staff Engineer, Lead Software Engineer, Technical Lead.

De-emphasise: support escalation ownership and highly customer-interrupt-driven delivery.

Evidence to strengthen: measurable pipeline improvement, scale and complexity of SQL work, architecture decisions, reliability or delivery outcomes.

## Technical Architect / ERP Consultant

**Promise:** Tanya converts complex operational and customer needs into practical, reliable enterprise solutions across ERP, data and integrations.

Lead with:
- ERP delivery for 75+ New Zealand and Australian clients.
- Discovery, workflow analysis, solution design, configuration and data migration.
- Integrations, reporting, SQL, C# and operational problem diagnosis.
- Documentation, training and repeatable implementation practices.
- Ability to bridge business context, customer needs and technical delivery.

Best-fit titles: ERP Technical Architect, Solution Architect, Principal ERP Consultant, Data and Integration Architect.

De-emphasise: presales, travel-heavy consulting and permanent multi-client escalation ownership.

Evidence to strengthen: solution scale, migration volumes, implementation duration reductions, reusable assets and stakeholder outcomes.

## Engineering Manager

**Promise:** Tanya builds capable, motivated engineering teams that deliver efficiently without relying on sustained overwork.

Lead with:
- Current leadership of six engineers.
- Delegation, planning, backlog shaping, prioritisation and capability development.
- High-trust culture, knowledge sharing and collaborative problem solving.
- Coaching, performance leadership and finding people's strengths.
- Highest-performing team in two consecutive performance reviews at Flintfox.
- Technical credibility across enterprise software, SQL, C# and delivery.

Best-fit titles: Engineering Manager, Software Development Manager, Technical Team Lead, small-team Head of Engineering.

De-emphasise: roles dominated by executive reporting, portfolio coordination or management of many squads.

Evidence to strengthen: retention, engagement, delivery predictability, capability progression and concrete examples of reducing overload.

## Selection rule

Choose the brief that matches the job's primary outcome. Use a secondary brief for supporting evidence, but do not combine all three into an unfocused summary.
