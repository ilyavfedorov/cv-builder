# Salary and take-home model

## Working thresholds

These are planning estimates for a New Zealand salary earner using the main progressive tax rates, the 2026-27 ACC earners' levy and no student-loan deduction.

| Gross base salary | Monthly after income tax and ACC | Monthly after tax, ACC and 3.5% employee KiwiSaver |
|---:|---:|---:|
| NZD 160,000 | NZD 9,548 | NZD 9,082 |
| NZD 170,000 | NZD 10,107 | NZD 9,611 |
| NZD 180,000 | NZD 10,665 | NZD 10,140 |
| NZD 190,000 | NZD 11,173 | NZD 10,619 |
| NZD 200,000 | NZD 11,682 | NZD 11,098 |

Approximate gross salary needed for NZD 10,000 monthly cash pay:

- **No KiwiSaver employee deduction:** NZD 168,088.
- **3.5% employee KiwiSaver deduction:** NZD 177,353.

Therefore:

- Keep NZD 160,000 as the absolute opportunity floor.
- Treat approximately NZD 168,100 as the cash-income floor when KiwiSaver is not deducted.
- Treat approximately NZD 177,400 as the income-goal floor at a 3.5% employee contribution.
- Ask whether advertised remuneration includes or excludes the employer KiwiSaver contribution.

## Assumptions and limitations

- Income-tax brackets from 1 April 2025: 10.5%, 17.5%, 30%, 33% and 39%.
- 2026-27 ACC earners' levy: 1.75% on earnings up to NZD 156,641.
- Default employee KiwiSaver contribution from 1 April 2026: 3.5%.
- Excludes student loan, tax credits, secondary income, bonuses, benefits, expense allowances and individual tax circumstances.
- Contract rates are not directly comparable with salary because leave, public holidays, gaps, insurance, accounting and GST treatment matter.
