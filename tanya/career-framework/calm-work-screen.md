# Calm-work verification framework

## Short recruiter screen

Use these questions before committing to a full interview process.

1. What base-salary range is approved, and is KiwiSaver additional to or included in it?
2. How many office days are expected in practice, and where is the team based?
3. Does this role join an on-call roster or own customer and production escalations?
4. Why is the role open, and how long did the previous person remain in it?
5. How many major priorities would this person normally own at the same time?
6. What proportion of a typical week is recurring meetings?
7. What does the hiring manager consider a sustainable workload for this role?

## Hiring-manager deep screen

Ask for recent examples rather than adjectives such as "flexible" or "supportive."

| Topic | Question | Healthy evidence | Warning evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ownership | What are the first 90-day outcomes, and what is explicitly outside the role? | Specific outcomes and boundaries | Everything is a priority |
| Work in progress | How many initiatives did the predecessor handle concurrently? | One major focus or explicit limits | Several unrelated workstreams |
| Interruptions | In the last month, how often did urgent work displace planned work? | Rare and handled by a defined path | Frequent executive/customer escalations |
| Meetings | What recurring meetings would I attend in a normal week? | Purposeful, limited calendar | Coordination occupies much of the week |
| Incidents | Who owns production incidents and after-hours decisions? | Dedicated rotation or operations function | Informal availability expected |
| Priorities | When two senior stakeholders disagree, who decides and how quickly? | Clear accountable decision-maker | Role-holder must negotiate continuously |
| Workload | What overtime has the team worked in the last six months, and why? | Exceptional and compensated/recovered | Normalised deadline pressure |
| Management | How are priorities removed when urgent work is added? | Explicit trade-offs | Work is added without anything stopping |
| Culture | Tell me about a recent time the team said no or delayed work. | Boundaries were respected | No concrete example |
| Vacancy | Why did the previous person leave? | Growth or clearly explained transition | Burnout, churn or evasiveness |

## Stop conditions

Decline regardless of title or salary when there is credible evidence of persistent overload, unclear ownership, routine escalation duty, several equally urgent workstreams, performative availability, or a manager who cannot describe how the team protects focus.

Record Unknown rather than assuming a positive answer. A calm-work claim without an example is not evidence.
