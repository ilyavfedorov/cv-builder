## What the market taught us

- **Requirements appearing repeatedly:**
  - Technical leadership combining hands-on engineering, architecture, mentoring and delivery direction.
  - C#/.NET, APIs, Azure/AWS, SQL, CI/CD and modern platform practices.
  - Ownership of technical standards, roadmaps, technical debt and platform health.
  - Stakeholder influence and communication across product, engineering and business groups.
  - For management roles, coaching, capability development, team health and delivery alignment.
  - Some roles require modern data platforms, MDM, Salesforce/nCino, full-stack breadth, embedded/IoT or non-.NET stacks.

- **Employers or sectors with credible calm-work signals:**
  - Established SaaS and mature product organisations with small autonomous teams.
  - Roles using language such as “work-life balance”, “flexible hours”, “quality and sustainability over shipping speed”, “clear ownership”, “remote-friendly”, “pragmatic” and “reduced cognitive load”.
  - The established SaaS Tech Lead .NET role had the strongest explicit calm-work evidence, although office days and operational expectations remain unknown.
  - Datacom’s mature Payroll platform and Re-Leased’s autonomous, remote-friendly squad are promising but require verification of workload, on-call and salary.
  - No employer has yet supplied verified recruiter intelligence in the dedicated file, so employer-level confidence remains provisional.

- **Recurring stress risks:**
  - On-call, incident response, production support, monitoring and service ownership.
  - Transformation programmes, greenfield work, rapid prototyping and high-growth or high-performance language.
  - Multiple squads, broad internal-consulting mandates, cross-functional coordination and competing priorities.
  - Presales, workshops, customer-facing delivery, travel and executive-facing influence.
  - Ambiguous office expectations and undisclosed meeting or concurrent-workload levels.

- **Salary and work-pattern intelligence:**
  - At least seven records showed an advertised base at or above the NZD 160,000 hard floor, with several in the NZD 170,000–199,000 range.
  - The preferred NZD 175,000–180,000 target is more reliably represented in the senior Tech Lead and Solutions Architect listings than in Engineering Manager listings.
  - Most September roles did not disclose salary, office frequency or employment-status detail. These remain verification gates, not reasons to assume suitability.
  - One Engineering Manager role explicitly failed the salary gate at NZD 135,000–145,000 and should not be pursued.
  - No reliable KiwiSaver treatment, contract rate, meeting-load or concurrent-priority evidence is recorded.

- **Reasons otherwise attractive roles were rejected or held:**
  - Resideo: explicitly on-site, failing Tanya’s location/work-pattern gate; also an IoT/embedded gap and undefined product direction.
  - Fusion5: presales, workshops, presentations, C-level influence and cross-sell responsibilities, despite flexible-work language.
  - Trade Me Lead Software Engineer: explicit on-call and incident management plus BAU, refactoring, features, prototypes and broad coordination.
  - Trade Me Principal Software Engineer: potentially high-stakes, high-demand, cross-squad and high-visibility scope.
  - Several architecture and principal roles: transformation, ambiguity, multiple stakeholders or organisational breadth created insufficient calm-work confidence.
  - Some roles remain held rather than rejected because salary, office days or operational expectations are unknown.

- **Feedback on Tanya’s positioning:**
  - The strongest apparent market fit is a senior hands-on technical-lead or principal role that combines .NET/SQL depth, architecture, mentoring and practical delivery improvement.
  - Tanya’s ERP, complex SQL, integration, legacy-system analysis, CI/CD modernisation and people-leadership evidence is unusually coherent for this segment.
  - The supplied data contains no direct employer feedback, so claims about recruiter or hiring-manager perception remain **inferred, not confirmed**.
  - Positioning should avoid presenting Tanya primarily as a generic transformation consultant, presales architect or high-availability service leader. Those role shapes repeatedly conflict with the calm-work requirement.
  - Management positioning remains credible, but should emphasise bounded team ownership, delegation, capability uplift and protection from overload rather than multi-squad delivery intensity.

## Strategy decision

- **Keep:**
  - The NZD 160,000 absolute base floor and preference for NZD 175,000–180,000 or better.
  - Remote or hybrid work with no more than three Auckland office days; North Shore and Auckland CBD remain acceptable locations.
  - Calm-work screening for ownership, interruptions, meetings, concurrent work in progress, on-call and incident response.
  - The primary positioning around senior .NET/SQL technical leadership, principal engineering, practical architecture and team capability uplift.
  - Selective pursuit of established SaaS/product roles with small autonomous teams and explicit sustainability or focus signals.

- **Change:**
  - Ask for salary, office days, on-call/incident expectations, meeting load and concurrent initiatives before investing in an application or interview process when these are absent.
  - Prioritise roles where Tanya’s combination of hands-on technical depth and people leadership is explicit, rather than generic “high-impact” principal roles.
  - Separate internal/platform architecture from customer-facing or presales architecture in searches and screening.
  - Record every recruiter conversation in `recruiter-intelligence.csv`; currently the file provides no usable market evidence.
  - Maintain a simple application/outcome log so future funnel measures distinguish screened, applied, screened by recruiter, interviewed, declined and rejected.

- **Stop:**
  - Stop pursuing roles below NZD 160,000, including the Serko Engineering Manager role.
  - Stop treating flexible-work or wellbeing language as sufficient calm-work evidence when the role also contains on-call, presales, workshops, travel, production support or multi-squad scope.
  - Stop advancing roles with explicit on-site requirements or more than three office days.
  - Stop broad, one-off upskilling in technologies that do not yet meet the three-role threshold.

- **One experiment for next month:**
  - Run a verification-first search experiment: select no more than five high-fit roles, contact the recruiter or hiring contact before applying where possible, and record five answers for each—base salary/package, exact office days, on-call/incident duties, meeting and concurrent-priority load, and reason the role is open. Advance only roles that preserve all hard gates and show credible calm-work evidence.

- **Changes required in `../search-profile.json`:**
  - **None.** Do not change the file. The current salary, location, employment-type and calm-work gates remain appropriate and must not be weakened.

A month with many screened vacancies but few fully verified matches is useful evidence: the search should become more selective and better instrumented, not broader at the expense of salary or sustainable working conditions.
