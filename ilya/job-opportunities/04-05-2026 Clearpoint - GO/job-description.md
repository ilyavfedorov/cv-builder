What we’re looking for
We're looking for someone who has moved from writing code to leading the people who write it
- and who has done both well. The ideal candidate brings:
● A strong software engineering foundation: You bring a strong software engineering
background and a passion for high-quality delivery. We’d love it if you’ve spent
significant time in the Microsoft stack, but we’re just as excited to talk to leaders from
other tech backgrounds who 'get' modern architecture. From microservices to
cloud-native patterns in AWS, Azure, or GCP, you’ve spent years in the trenches and
can speak the language of engineering because you’ve actually built the software.
● Engineering team leadership experience: You've led software engineering teams in
medium-to-large corporate environments - you understand how to operate inside
complexity, build high-performing teams, navigate stakeholder dynamics, and get
quality software shipped.
● Hands-on performance and line management: You've owned the full people
management lifecycle - setting expectations, coaching, performance reviews, and when
needed, managing people out. You're comfortable with the accountability that comes
