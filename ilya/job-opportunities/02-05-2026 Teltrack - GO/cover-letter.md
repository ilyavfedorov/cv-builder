May 2026

Dear Hiring Manager,

The Director of Software Engineering role at Teletrac Navman is the kind of job I am looking for.

Seven hundred thousand vehicles and assets in the field. Customers who feel downtime. That is where delivery predictability and sustainment stop being nice-to-have and become the core of the work.

Operating rhythms and intake systems are things I have built from scratch. At Mindhive I designed a full delivery cadence: structured intake, triage, and prioritisation across engineering, product, and customer success. I added flow metrics so we had a live picture of demand and throughput. At Tradify I did the same across multiple teams and brought product into one shared view of flow. The goal in both cases was the same: predictable delivery, less reactive churn.

On sustainment, I have owned the hard side of it. At Serko I cut error rates on critical pathways by 90%. At Tradify I traced systemic platform misuse to the root cause and reduced spamming incidents by about 75%. At Mindhive I built follow-the-sun support to keep production covered across time zones. I know what it takes to reduce recurring customer pain, not just manage it.

For stakeholders I work at senior level. At Mindhive I sit in SLT and board discussions on capacity, security posture, and organisational design. At Te Kete Hono I owned the board relationship across three SaaS products and through a full business sale and due diligence. I can translate between engineering and executives without losing either side.

AI in engineering workflows is current work for me. I lead engineering at Mindhive, an AI and ML company. I have driven adoption of AI-assisted practices to improve how the team delivers, not just what it ships.

I have not led a mobile engineering team as a primary focus. My background is backend, full stack, and cloud-native with strong web frontend. I led a zero-downtime migration from AngularJS to React at Tradify. I would build mobile capability by working closely with strong mobile engineers and managers. IoT and telematics would also be new to me as a product domain. I have managed high-availability, data-heavy SaaS at scale, which gives me the adjacent base.

What draws me to this role is the operational reality of it. Connected assets in the field, customers who depend on uptime, and AI in both the product and the engineering organisation. If that sounds like the profile you need, I am happy to talk.
