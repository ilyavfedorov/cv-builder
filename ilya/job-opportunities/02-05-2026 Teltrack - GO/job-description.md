Teletrac Navman is looking for a Director of Software Engineering to lead and evolve our software and mobile engineering capability across core and customer‑facing platforms. This is a hands‑on, high‑accountability leadership role for someone who thrives in complex environments and is ready to take end‑to‑end ownership of engineering outcomes, not just team coordination.

If you are energised by delivery excellence, system reliability, modern engineering practices, and the practical application of AI across engineering workflows, this role offers genuine scope and influence.

The Opportunity

As Director of Software Engineering, you will own the performance, reliability, and delivery of multiple engineering teams working across our core platform and mobile products. You will partner closely with Product, the PMO, and senior stakeholders to translate strategy into execution, while modernising sustainment and embedding AI‑enabled engineering practices.

This role sits at the intersection of people leadership, technical strategy, and operational excellence. Success will be measured by delivery predictability, customer experience, system quality, and the strength of the engineering organisation you build.

What You’ll Be Accountable For

Engineering Leadership & Delivery

Full ownership of end‑to‑end software delivery, reliability, and engineering execution

Translating company and product strategy into measurable engineering outcomes

Establishing strong operating rhythms, standards, and accountability across teams

Acting as the escalation point for complex delivery and technical challenges

Sustainment & Customer Impact

Ownership of customer support ticket (SPT) lifecycle, backlog health, and SLAs

Reducing recurring issues through root‑cause analysis and systemic fixes

Driving high‑quality outcomes across frontend, services, and mobile platforms

Backlog, Change & Execution Management

Leadership of SPT and Change Request intake, prioritisation, and delivery

Partnering with Product to ensure clarity of requirements and acceptance criteria

Overseeing release planning, risk mitigation, and multi‑workstream delivery

Projects & Cross‑Functional Execution

Engineering ownership of major projects from inception to delivery

Providing credible effort estimates, delivery plans, and risk management

Coordinating work across firmware, data, reporting, and platform teams

AI & Engineering Innovation

Introducing AI‑driven capabilities to improve productivity, quality, and support operations

Driving adoption of AI‑assisted development tools and workflows

Partnering with Product and Data teams to embed intelligent features where appropriate

People Leadership

Leading and developing engineering managers and senior engineers

Building a high‑performing, scalable organisation with strong succession

Creating a culture of ownership, technical excellence, and continuous improvement

Metrics & Continuous Improvement

Defining and owning KPIs across delivery, quality, reliability, and throughput

Using data to drive improvement in engineering performance and outcomes

What We’re Looking For

This role requires senior‑level engineering leadership experience. It will not suit hands‑off managers, delivery coordinators, or candidates without large‑scale ownership.

Essential experience

12+ years in software engineering, including 8+ years in senior engineering leadership roles

Proven leadership of multi‑team or platform‑level engineering organisations

Strong background in mobile, backend, and/or full‑stack systems

Experience integrating AI/ML into products or engineering workflows

Demonstrated ownership of delivery, operations, and production environments

Experience working across Agile, Hybrid, and Waterfall models at scale

Strong stakeholder leadership with Product, PMO, and executive teams

Experience delivering SaaS or large‑scale platforms

Highly regarded

Strong understanding of DevOps, CI/CD, and cloud‑native architectures

Experience with high‑availability, customer‑facing platforms

Background in sustainment or support‑heavy engineering environments

Exposure to IoT, telematics, or device‑ingested data platforms

What Success Looks Like

You operate at a true Director level—balancing near‑term delivery with long‑term technical strategy. You make data‑driven decisions, set clear expectations, and act as a multiplier across people and systems. You communicate effectively with senior leaders and create clarity where others see complexity.
